How it works

1. InputSoil N, P, K, pH plus temperature, humidity and rainfall.
2. ModelInputs are compared with typical growing profiles of 15 crops.
3. OutputTop 3 crops ranked by how closely conditions match.

This demo uses a nearest-profile matcher running in your browser. To use the Random Forest from your Python project, expose it through a Flask or FastAPI endpoint and call it from this page.
